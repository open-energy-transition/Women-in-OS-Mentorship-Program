# Women in Energy Open Source Mentorship Programme

## Learn. Collaborate. Contribute.

<img width="3185" height="476" alt="WIEOS_Stacked Logo_colour" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7aae552e-f9bf-4b9d-b1b2-b65414716271" />

Welcome to the Women in Energy Open Source (WIEOS) Mentorship Programme!

We are a global educational initiative supporting women in gaining practical skills, building professional connections, developing and hands-on experience in open-source energy modelling. The programme offers opportunities to learn, collaborate, and engage with open-source energy communities.

The Women in Energy Open Source Mentorship Programme is supported by:

[Energy Access Explorer](https://www.energyaccessexplorer.org/) ·
[Open Energy Transition (OET)](https://openenergytransition.org/) ·
[Centre for Net Zero](https://www.centrefornetzero.org/) ·
[PyPSA meets Earth](https://pypsa-meets-earth.github.io/) ·
[LF Energy](https://lfenergy.org/)

## Explore the open-source ecosystem

This repository serves as a navigation hub connecting participants with open-source projects, learning resources, and supporting communities.
